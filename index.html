Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming abandoned and surrendered animals across the Twin Cities area. We believe every dog, cat, and small animal deserves a safe home, proper medical care, and a family who loves them. Through adoption, fostering, and community education, we work every day to reduce animal homelessness in our region.

Our Impact

Since opening our doors, our organization has made a measurable difference for animals in need throughout the Twin Cities.

  • 1,240 animals placed in loving homes
  • 85 active foster families this year
  • 310 volunteers currently on our roster
  • 92% adoption success rate

How You Can Help

There are many ways to support our mission, whether you have a little time or a lot.

  • Adopt a pet and give them a permanent home
  • Foster an animal temporarily while they wait for adoption
  • Volunteer your time at our shelter or at community events
  • Donate supplies, funds, or professional services
  • Spread the word by sharing our adoptable pets with friends and family
