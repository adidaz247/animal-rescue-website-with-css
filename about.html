Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue began as a small foster network run out of a handful of volunteers' homes. Over the years, that grassroots effort grew into a full shelter and community resource, but our founding belief has never changed: every animal deserves a safe place to land and a family to love them.

Stories like this one remind us why our work matters — every adoption creates a new bond between a person and an animal who needed each other.

A Message From Our Shelter Director

Core Values

  • Compassion: We treat every animal with patience, kindness, and respect.
  • Transparency: We are honest with adopters, donors, and our community about our work and our needs.
  • Community: We believe lasting change happens when neighbors work together.
  • Responsibility: We are committed to responsible, humane animal care practices.
