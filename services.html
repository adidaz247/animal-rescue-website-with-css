Adoption Process

Adopting from Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a simple, guided process designed to make sure every animal is matched with the right family.

  1. Browse our list of adoptable animals online or visit the shelter in person
  2. Submit an adoption application
  3. Meet the animal and complete a brief compatibility visit
  4. Attend an adoption counseling session with our staff
  5. Finalize paperwork and take your new companion home

Foster Program

Fostering gives animals a safe, temporary home while they wait for adoption, and it is one of the most impactful ways to support our mission.

Foster families receive all necessary supplies, food, and veterinary support from our organization. Foster periods typically range from two to eight weeks, depending on the animal's needs.

Volunteer Roles

Our volunteers are the heart of our organization. We welcome help from community members of all skill levels.

  • Animal Care Volunteer: Assist with feeding, walking, and socializing shelter animals.
  • Event Volunteer: Help staff adoption events and community outreach booths.
  • Administrative Volunteer: Support the office with data entry, phone calls, and scheduling.
  • Transport Volunteer: Drive animals to veterinary appointments and foster homes.

Interested in joining us? Fill out our volunteer interest form.
